# Session Method

## Session contract

Begin each session with:

- one concept;
- one observable learner outcome;
- the prerequisite being relied upon;
- the connection to the active track or bridge.

## Diagnostic session

1. Collect confidence ratings without references.
2. Select three to five probes near the claimed boundary.
3. Prefer a derivation, distinction, or small problem over vocabulary recall.
4. Stop once the earliest missing dependency is located.
5. Record the strongest demonstrated foundation and first lesson.

A rating is a hypothesis. The probe supplies the evidence.

## Teaching session

1. **Retrieve:** Ask for the prerequisite or current mental model.
2. **Orient:** State what the concept lets the learner explain or calculate.
3. **Intuit:** Give a concrete or geometric model and identify its limits.
4. **Formalize:** Define every object, type, domain, and notation used.
5. **Derive:** Walk through the central argument without hiding the decisive
   step behind a slogan.
6. **Model:** Work one representative example.
7. **Attempt:** Give the learner one meaningful step and wait for the response.
8. **Respond:** Identify what was correct, locate the first error, and choose a
   hint, repair, or extension.
9. **Check:** Ask for reconstruction or transfer to a slightly changed case.
10. **Record:** Update progress with only the mastery actually demonstrated.

## Mastery evidence

Use these levels when describing progress:

| Level | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Exposed | Recognizes the idea after explanation |
| Assisted | Completes a standard step with hints or references |
| Independent | Solves or derives the standard case without help |
| Transfer | Applies the idea to a changed or unfamiliar case |
| Fluent | Explains, proves, implements, or critiques it reliably |

Completion normally requires independent reconstruction. A bridge prerequisite
requires the exact evidence named by that bridge.

## Feedback

- Lead with the mathematical or conceptual fact that decides the issue.
- Distinguish notation mistakes from conceptual mistakes.
- Correct the first invalid step before discussing downstream consequences.
- Preserve productive struggle by offering the smallest useful hint.
- Re-explain with a different representation when the first representation
  failed: algebraic, geometric, computational, physical, or protocol-level.

## Session close

State:

- what the learner demonstrated;
- what remains in progress;
- any question worth preserving;
- the single best next step.
