# CI Belts And Rings

Snapshot: Path of Exile 1, patch 3.29, verified 2026-08-10.

Plan both rings and the belt together. These slots commonly solve attributes
and resistances so the body armour, helmet, gloves, and weapon can spend affixes
on rarer local or damage modifiers.

## Solve The Whole Set

Before buying or crafting, calculate the final three-slot requirements:

```text
needed attribute = requirement + desired buffer - contribution from stable gear
needed resistance = target uncapped value - contribution from stable gear
```

Allocate suffix roles across both rings and the belt. Do not require every slot
to be independently balanced.

## Crystal Belt Role

A Crystal Belt supplies an implicit ES roll, can roll ordinary flat ES as a
prefix, and can carry high elemental resistance suffixes. Check the implicit
before paying to finish the explicit modifiers.

Ordinary belts do not all naturally roll the standard Intelligence suffix.
Deafening Essence of Spite can guarantee an Intelligence suffix on a belt, but
it rerolls the other non-fractured explicit modifiers. Crusader influence can
offer increased maximum ES at the cost of a different pool and plan.

Common belt priorities:

1. strong implicit and flat ES;
2. missing elemental resistances and useful overcap;
3. Strength or another attribute the complete gear set needs;
4. an open bench affix for the final deterministic repair;
5. influence only when its PoB value justifies the crafting complexity.

## Ring Roles

Rings can solve combinations of:

- flat ES prefix;
- Intelligence and other attribute suffixes;
- elemental resistances;
- cast speed or build-specific damage;
- crafted attributes, resistance, or another final repair;
- a useful base implicit whose current version is verified.

Essence of Woe can guarantee an ES modifier and Essence of Spite can guarantee
Intelligence, but the rest of the item remains a random reforge. Choose the
Essence based on the hardest required affix, not the most attractive isolated
number.

## Buy Before Crafting When Appropriate

Because rings combine widely demanded attributes and resistances, finished
items can be cheaper than forcing one exact three-suffix set. Search using:

1. hard minimum attribute and resistance filters;
2. one or two flexible weighted damage or ES filters;
3. an open prefix or suffix when a bench finish matters;
4. total price of the complete three-slot solution.

Verify the post-swap totals in PoB. An apparently superior belt is not an
upgrade when replacing it forces Intelligence onto an expensive helmet or
gloves suffix.

## Crafting Stop Conditions

Stop and price the intermediate item when it hits a combination another build
may value, especially high attributes plus resistances or high ES plus cast
speed. Sell rather than force a narrow CI finish when the market values the
current result more highly than the expected finished item.
