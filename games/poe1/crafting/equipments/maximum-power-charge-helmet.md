# Maximum-Power-Charge Hybrid Helmet

Snapshot: Path of Exile 1, patch 3.29, verified 2026-08-10.

Use this guide for a hybrid Armour/ES helmet whose special target is +1 maximum
Power Charge. Read the current influence and affix tables before crafting.

## Influence And Slot Cost

The target is a Warlord-influenced prefix available from item level 75 with low
base weight. It occupies one of three prefix slots. A finished local
defense plan therefore has only two remaining prefixes, commonly flat Armour/ES
and increased Armour/ES.

A conventionally influenced helmet cannot use normal Eldritch implicits. Price
the lost implicit options before deciding that +1 maximum Power Charge is the
best whole-build helmet.

## Base Checklist

- hybrid Armour/ES base with acceptable requirements and base percentile;
- item level high enough for +1 maximum Power Charge and the intended defense,
  Intelligence, resistance, and recharge tiers;
- Warlord influence present before rolling the target;
- quality applied before evaluating displayed defenses;
- prefix budget reserved for the charge and two local defense families.

## Low-Budget Isolation Route

1. Scour to normal only when no valuable state would be destroyed.
2. Transmute and Alteration-roll for +1 maximum Power Charge.
3. If the magic item has one modifier, use Augmentation only after listing the
   eligible opposite-side outcomes and accepting the random result.
4. Regal once the magic state is worth preserving.
5. Stop and recount the rare item before any Annulment, Exalted, or bench step.

This route preserves the rare charge hit cheaply but does not make a top local
defense Regal likely. Accuracy, resistance, or another random suffix may be
usable; removing it is not automatically safe. A regular Annulment can remove
the charge.

## Finish Or Restart

Finish with multimod only when the natural affixes plus available bench crafts
produce a meaningful upgrade. `Can have up to 3 Crafted Modifiers` consumes a
suffix and counts as one crafted modifier, so count the two remaining crafted
slots before promising one prefix plus multiple suffixes.

Restart or use a higher-budget influence method when:

- the only natural good modifier is the charge and bench values cannot reach
  the defensive target;
- the Regal fills a required prefix with a weak modifier;
- resistances or attributes make the completed item unusable;
- a comparable finished helmet costs less than the expected remaining process.

## Strong Finished State

Prioritise:

- +1 maximum Power Charge prefix;
- two strong local Armour/ES prefixes;
- required resistance or Intelligence suffixes;
- useful recharge or another build-specific suffix;
- one deterministic bench modifier when it completes the item.

Compare the actual charge's PoB damage and utility against a non-influenced
helmet with stronger defenses and Eldritch implicits. Do not value the charge in
isolation.
