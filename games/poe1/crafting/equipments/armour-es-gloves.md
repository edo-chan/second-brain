# Armour/ES Gloves For CI

Snapshot: Path of Exile 1, patch 3.29, verified 2026-08-10.

Use this guide for hybrid Armour/Energy Shield gloves on a CI build. Read the
shared crafting and ilvl 86 references first.

## Base And Prefixes

Choose a strong hybrid base such as Paladin Gloves when the Strength and
Intelligence requirements fit. Item level 86 is sufficient, but gloves do not
receive the body-armour-only 101-110% local defense tier.

The ideal ordinary local defense set is:

- top flat Armour plus ES prefix;
- top increased Armour and ES prefix;
- hybrid increased Armour and ES plus stun/block recovery prefix.

A top percentage roll does not rescue a low flat defense roll. Compare final
displayed Armour and ES, not the percentage tier alone.

## Suffix Plan

Reserve suffixes before rolling:

- exact missing elemental resistance and required overcap;
- Intelligence only when the final gear set needs or values it;
- recharge or another build-specific utility modifier;
- an open bench suffix when deterministic flexibility is worth more than a
  random natural modifier.

Do the full attribute and resistance subtraction before assigning these roles.
Do not force Dexterity onto gloves unless the PoB proves the final set needs it.

## Eldritch Implicits

For a compatible non-influenced pair, price useful Eldritch implicits separately
from explicit affixes. Chaos DoT multiplier, suppression, exposure, or another
implicit is valuable only when the skill and defensive plan use it.

Conventional influence prevents normal Eldritch implicits. Decide whether a
special influenced modifier is worth losing the Eldritch plan before crafting.

## Crafting Routes

1. Start with a verified base percentile, item level, quality, and influence
   state.
2. Use a defense-targeting method such as a Dense Fossil or an Essence whose
   guaranteed modifier belongs in the finished item.
3. Keep only a prefix set that beats the equipped gloves after the removed
   attributes and resistances are subtracted.
4. If prefixes are strong and the item is compatible, use correct Eldritch
   dominance to work suffixes.
5. Finish one deterministic missing resistance or utility modifier at the
   bench when that produces a complete item.
6. Roll implicits after the explicit plan is stable, unless the implicits are
   required to enable safe Eldritch manipulation.

## Keep Thresholds

Define thresholds from the equipped item. For a hybrid defensive glove, report
Armour and ES separately, then the character-level delta. Treat roughly 600+
Armour and 180+ ES as a useful strong-item search band, not an automatic keep;
the required resistance, implicits, and open affix can outweigh a modest local
defense difference.

Stop after a material prefix hit. Recount combined modifiers before assuming a
third open prefix or assigning a tier.
