# Build Damage

Read `intake.md` first and require the latest PoB for personalized advice.
Identify the skill's real damage component before ranking modifiers.

## Classify The Damage

Determine whether the build scales:

- attack or spell;
- hit, damage over time, or ailment;
- elemental, physical, chaos, or conversion;
- critical or non-critical;
- projectile, area, duration, channelled, minion, totem, trap, or mine tags;
- one primary skill or several contributions included in Full DPS.

Do not credit a modifier merely because its wording resembles the skill. Confirm
the skill tags and whether the gem explicitly allows an unusual modifier, such
as spell damage, to apply to its DoT.

## Audit Damage In Order

1. **Skill setup:** correct active gem, selected skill part, gem level, quality,
   links, support compatibility, and socketed-item modifiers.
2. **Uptime:** realistic curses, exposure, penetration, Wither, charges,
   flasks, nearby-enemy conditions, and boss target.
3. **Base scaling:** weapon damage for attacks, gem level or base damage for
   spells, and minion level where relevant.
4. **Multipliers:** damage-over-time multiplier, critical multiplier, more
   modifiers, resistance reduction, and other multiplicative layers.
5. **Speed and application:** attack/cast speed, cooldown, projectile count,
   duration, area, accuracy, and time spent moving.
6. **Opportunity cost:** passive points, sockets, reservation, defenses,
   attributes, and currency.

Fix a broken link, disabled aura, wrong skill selection, or impossible
configuration before buying gear.

## Honest PoB Comparison

- Duplicate the baseline and change one thing at a time.
- Keep enemy, configuration, flask, charge, and skill settings identical.
- Report mapping and boss damage separately when their uptime assumptions
  differ.
- Distinguish peak damage from sustained damage and damage while moving.
- Check mana, life, ES, rage, or other resource cost at the new action speed.
- Do not count a temporary effect at permanent uptime without a generation
  mechanism.

## Chaos Spell DoT Priorities

For Essence Drain/Contagion and similar non-ailment Chaos spell DoT builds,
test these families in PoB:

- active skill gem levels and relevant support-gem levels;
- Chaos damage-over-time multiplier and generic DoT multiplier;
- increased Chaos damage and other increases that apply;
- spell damage only when the skill explicitly applies it to its DoT;
- enemy Chaos resistance reduction, curses, and realistic Wither uptime;
- duration, area, and cast speed for application and clear quality;
- secondary single-target skills only when their uptime is realistic.

Flat added spell damage normally does not scale a non-hit DoT component. Cast
speed can improve application or channelled secondary damage without directly
multiplying an already-applied DoT; verify the selected skill calculation.

## Rank Damage Upgrades

For each option, report:

- baseline and resulting sustained boss damage;
- absolute and percentage gain;
- cost per meaningful gain;
- defensive, socket, attribute, reservation, and playability losses;
- whether the gain depends on a conditional configuration;
- the next bottleneck created by the change.

Prefer upgrades that preserve the user's defensive floor. A large tooltip or
peak-DPS increase is not automatically better than a smaller reliable increase.
