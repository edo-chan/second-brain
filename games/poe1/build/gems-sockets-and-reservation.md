# Gems, Sockets, And Reservation

Read `intake.md` first and require the latest PoB. Socket and reservation advice
must account for every enabled group, item-granted support, attribute
requirement, and the function lost when a gem is removed.

## Inventory Every Socket Group

For each group, record:

- item slot and number of links;
- active skill or aura it serves;
- enabled gems, levels, quality, and alternate versions;
- item-granted supports or socketed-gem levels;
- attribute and resource requirements;
- whether the group is always active, automated, triggered, or weapon-swapped.

Do not treat a disabled gem as current defense or damage. Do not assume the PoB
main group is the skill the user considers primary without confirming it.

## Protect Essential Functions

Classify groups by job:

1. primary single-target damage;
2. clear or application;
3. auras and reservation;
4. curses and enemy debuffs;
5. guard and defensive automation;
6. movement and escape;
7. recovery, utility, or charge generation;
8. leveling or optional gems.

When adding Steelskin, another guard, or an aura, name the exact gem displaced
and explain how its old job will be handled. Replacing Phase Run, a blink, curse
application, or automation is not a free socket.

## Evaluate A Link Change

- Verify the support applies to the selected skill part.
- Compare damage under the same configuration and resource uptime.
- Check mana, life, ES, or other cost at the resulting action rate.
- Include gem-level and quality breakpoints.
- Check whether a support changes duration, area, application speed, or another
  playability property not captured by one DPS number.
- Confirm colours and attribute requirements are practical on the target base.

## Reservation Changes

Before adding an aura or reservation skill:

- calculate unreserved resource after the complete change;
- preserve enough resource for the real skill cost and automation cadence;
- verify reservation efficiency sources and their conditions;
- account for Enlighten, item modifiers, and gem level;
- test whether losing another aura, banner, stance, or utility effect defeats
  the intended gain.

Report both the PoB benefit and the socket, attribute, colour, and reservation
cost. A theoretical aura that cannot be sustained is not an upgrade.
