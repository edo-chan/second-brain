# CI Chaos Spell DoT Build Guide

Use this reference for Essence Drain/Contagion and related Chaos spell DoT
characters using Chaos Inoculation. Require the current PoB before applying it
to a specific build.

## Core Build Model

- Life is set aside; Energy Shield is the primary hit pool.
- Chaos immunity removes the ordinary need to gear for chaos resistance.
- Intelligence can satisfy requirements and globally scale ES.
- Chaos spell DoT damage usually values gem levels, applicable DoT multiplier,
  applicable increases, enemy resistance reduction, curses, and Wither.
- Contagion and area/duration mechanics primarily support clear and spread;
  verify which skill owns single-target damage.

## Defensive Priorities

1. stable ES pool from strong local ES bases and global scaling;
2. capped elemental resistances with practical overcap;
3. reliable physical mitigation such as armour, endurance charges, or another
   verified layer;
4. attack and spell defense through block, suppression, or the layers the
   chosen weapon setup can actually support;
5. ES regeneration, recharge, leech, recovery rate, or gain-on-block with
   realistic uptime;
6. stun, freeze, shock, ignite, bleed, corrupted blood, and curse handling;
7. a guard skill and movement setup that fit the available sockets.

A staff prevents shield-specific defenses and may rule out mechanics that
require a shield. Do not recommend them solely because another version of the
build uses one.

## Damage Priorities

Test each family in the current PoB:

1. active Chaos spell gem levels;
2. Chaos DoT multiplier and generic DoT multiplier;
3. spell or Chaos increases that the skill actually applies;
4. Despair, Wither, and other enemy resistance effects at realistic uptime;
5. support-gem levels and compatibility;
6. cast speed, duration, and area for application and playability;
7. a secondary single-target Chaos skill when sockets and uptime justify it.

Do not use flat added spell damage as a default DoT upgrade. Keep Wither stacks,
charges, curses, and flasks honest in boss comparisons.

## Typical Gear Roles

- **Staff:** gem levels, spell damage, Chaos DoT multiplier, and cast speed.
- **Body armour:** largest local ES contribution plus useful recharge or
  resistances when suffixes allow.
- **Helmet:** local Armour/ES, Intelligence, resistance, and a build-specific
  special modifier only when its influence tradeoff is worthwhile.
- **Gloves:** local Armour/ES, resistance or Intelligence, and useful implicits
  when the item remains Eldritch-compatible.
- **Boots:** movement, defenses, resistances, ailment handling, and attributes.
- **Belt and rings:** remaining attributes and resistances, flat or increased
  ES, cast speed, and flexible bench space.
- **Flasks:** fill a named defense, mobility, recovery, or ailment gap with
  credible uptime.

## Socket And Reservation Pressure

Before adding a guard skill, aura, automation, curse, or movement gem:

1. list every active socket group and its purpose;
2. verify attribute and reservation cost at the intended gem level;
3. identify the exact gem being removed;
4. test both damage and defensive uptime after the swap;
5. prefer one reliable guard or movement setup over several disabled gems.

Never recommend replacing a movement or automation skill without describing
the lost function and how the build will perform it afterward.
